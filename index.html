What sets Cliffer apart

Three commitments run through every frame we manufacture, from raw material to final seal.

Purity of Quality

Our uPVC profiles are sourced from top-tier suppliers and undergo stringent quality assurance. Every batch is tested for consistency, durability, and performance.

Exquisite Finishing

Each unit is meticulously crafted for smooth operation, tight seals, and a flawless look — elegant interiors, superior weather resistance, lasting appeal.

Fully Reinforced with GI

We embed galvanised iron bars — typically 1mm to 2mm thick — within every frame and sash, enhancing stability, security, and resistance to warping.
